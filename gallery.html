The examples, in the order to learn them

Every example world in examples/, as a route: what each one teaches, what it needs first, how big it is, how to run it, and one thing to change and predict before you run it again. Each opens in the workshop with Start this world over on the card at the right; the manual explains the parts.

First steps · Things that talk · Numbers and data · Houses and state · Games · Infinity · Meta

First steps

One visible change, a small robot, a predictable result.

1 🔀 swap numbers

A robot does one thing to what it is given, and stops when its thought no longer fits.

small — 1 robot, the longest 6 steps

Run: Give the leaning scale to the Swap robot.

Change and predict: Give it the OTHER scale, the one already the right way round. Predict: does it run, and why not?

Open in the workshop Start over is on the card at the right.

2 🏃 moving behaviours

A behaviour is a letter to a bird: [move | across | 1/60] every round, and the star slides.

small — 1 robot, the longest 2 steps · needs 🔀 swap

Run: Set Speed to 8× and give the work box to the Mover.

Change and predict: Pick up the step, type a minus. Predict which way the star goes — then make the number bigger.

3 🪐 step-and-turn-planet lessons
